# KPI dashboard definitions

| KPI | Baseline | Target | Data source | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic sessions | Capture after GA4 live | +30% in 90 days | GA4 | Weekly |
| Organic queries in top 10 | Capture after GSC live | 2x in 6 months | GSC | Monthly |
| Average position (branded + ICUMSA queries) | Capture after GSC live | Improving | GSC | Monthly |
| CTR | Capture after GSC live | > 3% on target queries | GSC | Monthly |
| LCP / INP / CLS | Capture after GSC CWV | LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS < 0.1 | GSC / PSI | Weekly |
| `generate_lead` events | 0 until GA4 ID set | Track weekly; set a numeric target after 30 days of data | GA4 | Weekly |
| Indexed Insights + FAQ URLs | 0 before this release | All published URLs in sitemap indexed | GSC | Weekly for 4 weeks |
| AI citations | Manual spot-check | Record monthly | ChatGPT / Perplexity / AI Overviews | Monthly |

Baselines cannot be invented. Fill the Baseline column the week GA4 and GSC are connected.
